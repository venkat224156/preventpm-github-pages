OUR PEST CONTROL SERVICES

Rodents

Rodents are mammals of the order Rodentia, which are characterized by a single pair of continuously growing incisors in each of the upper and lower jaws.

Termites

Termites are known as silent destroyers because of their ability to chew through wood, flooring and even wallpaper undetected.

Ants

They are social insects, which means they live in large colonies or groups.

Wasps

Unlike bees, wasps can sting repeatedly and often become a nuisance around homes.

Spiders

Spiders are air-breathing arthropods that have eight legs and can quickly spread in attics and crawlspaces.

Bees

There are about 20,000 different species of bees in the world and many require careful relocation or treatment.

Centipedes

Centipedes are predatory arthropods belonging to the class Chilopoda and thrive in dark, moist areas.

Mosquitos

A mosquito is any member of a group of about 3,500 species of small insects belonging to the order Diptera.

Flies

There are more than 120,000 species of flies worldwide with about 18,000 found in North America.
