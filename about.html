OUR PEST CONTROL SERVICES

Rodents

Rodents are mammals of the order Rodentia, which are characterized by a single pair of continuously growing incisors in each of the upper and lower jaws.

Termites

Termites are known as silent destroyers because of their ability to chew through wood, flooring and even wallpaper undetected.

Ants

They are social insects that live in large colonies or groups and can quickly become a recurring household problem.

Wasps

Unlike bees, wasps can sting repeatedly and frequently build nests near homes, patios, and rooflines.

Spiders

Spiders are air-breathing arthropods with eight legs and are among the most common pests seen around homes.

Bees

There are about 20,000 different species of bees in the world and many require safe, careful removal strategies.

Centipedes

Centipedes are elongated arthropods with one pair of legs per body segment and thrive in moist environments.

Mosquitos

A mosquito is any member of a group of about 3,500 species of small insects belonging to the order Diptera.

Flies

There are more than 120,000 species of flies worldwide and many are attracted to food waste and moisture.
